Our point of view

Made for your own rhythm.

Raiko brings East Asian street style into everyday wardrobes without losing individuality.

Streetwear with a closer connection.

Raiko began with a simple idea: clothing inspired by Japanese and Korean aesthetics should feel personal, wearable, and within reach. We select graphic tees, relaxed silhouettes, and expressive details that work far beyond a single trend.

Our collections move between Tokyo's layered street culture and Seoul's clean, confident styling. Each piece is chosen to mix easily with what you already own, so the final look still feels like you.
